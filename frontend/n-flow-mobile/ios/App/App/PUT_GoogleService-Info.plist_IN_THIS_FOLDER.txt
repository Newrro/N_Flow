Download "GoogleService-Info.plist" from your Firebase project
(the iOS app you register) and add it to THIS folder:

    ios/App/App/GoogleService-Info.plist

IMPORTANT: Don't just copy it on disk — in Xcode, drag it into
the "App" target in the left sidebar and tick "Copy items if
needed" so Xcode actually bundles it. Otherwise the app won't
find it at runtime.

You can delete this .txt file after you add the real one.

Full step-by-step is in:  ios/IOS_SETUP.md
